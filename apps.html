Glyphy

The Essential SF Symbol Picker for macOS

Glyphy is a lightweight, native macOS utility designed to streamline your design and development workflow. It provides instant access to the entire SF Symbols library, allowing you to search, preview, and insert icons directly into your projects with a single keystroke. Whether you need a raw string, an SVG, or a PNG, Glyphy handles the heavy lifting so you can stay in your creative flow without ever leaving your IDE or design tool.

  • Global Hotkey Access: Summon the picker instantly from any application with a custom shortcut.
  • Versatile Export Formats: Effortlessly copy symbols as Text, SVG, or high-quality PNG.
  • Smart Search & Categories: Find the perfect icon in seconds with intuitive filtering and symbol categorization.
  • Native & Lightweight: Built with Swift for peak performance and a seamless, aesthetic macOS experience.
